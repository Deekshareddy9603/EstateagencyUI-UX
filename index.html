Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about buying, selling, and renting with EstatePro.

How does EstatePro verify property listings?
Every listing undergoes a rigorous 4-step check: government land registry title deeds audit, physical on-site inspection by our field engineers, verification of municipal building permits (including RERA status), and ownership cross-referencing to ensure zero encumbrances or disputes.
Can EstatePro help me get competitive home loan rates?
Yes, we partner with top national and private financial institutions to bring you pre-negotiated home loan interest rates, zero processing-fee offers on select properties, and dedicated mortgage counselors.
Are there any hidden brokerage fees for buyers?
No. Transparent pricing is core to EstatePro. For residential buyer listings tagged direct or zero-brokerage, you pay zero hidden commission. Any standard advisory tiers are disclosed upfront before engagement.
Can I schedule a 3D virtual tour before visiting in person?
Absolutely! Over 80% of our featured properties support interactive 3D Matterport walkthroughs, and you can also request a live video walkthrough guided by our on-site property specialist.
What documents are required to initiate property registration?
Key documents include PAN cards and ID proofs of buyer/seller, original title/sale deed chain, latest property tax receipts, encumbrance certificate (EC), occupancy certificate (if applicable), and approved layout plans.
