Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about renting through RealEstate.

What documents are required to rent a property?

Standard government ID proofs (Aadhaar, Passport, or PAN card), employer verification letter or recent pay slips, and permanent address verification.

How are security deposits handled?

Security deposits are typically between 2 to 6 months of rent and are documented legally in your agreement, fully refundable upon move-out clearance.

Are utility bills included in monthly rent?

Electricity, water meter, and piped gas charges are paid by the tenant according to individual consumption. Society maintenance fees may be included or billed separately depending on the listing.

What is the standard lock-in & notice period?

Standard rental agreements have an 11-month tenure with a 3 to 6-month lock-in period, along with a 30-day mutual advance notice requirement before departure.
